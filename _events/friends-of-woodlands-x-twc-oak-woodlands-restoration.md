TWC will be joining Friends of Oak Woodlands to help SF Recreation and Parks in restoring the native oak woodlands in Golden Gate Park. This is a fun opportunity to be in nature, to help improve our local ecology, to learn more about the land we live in, and to do all of this through vigorous physical activity!

Be punctual we'll meet at the Horseshoe Courts but be moving soon after, please wear closed-toed shows and come ready to work and sweat (tools and gloves will be provided).
